The Project

CERNAL — Compiler-like Engine for RNA Logic

CERNAL was developed by the iGEM 2026 Tel Aviv University (TAU) team as part of the International Genetically Engineered Machine (iGEM) competition — the world's largest synthetic biology community. CERNAL grew out of the team's goal to make designing RNA-based logical circuits accessible to any lab, turning what used to be months of complicated manual circuit design into a simple and quick process.

Empowering scientists with the ability to design RNA-trigger-based systems that sense their environment and respond only when the right conditions are met would accelerate the development of RNA-based synthetic biology solutions, from smart therapeutics to complex biosensors.
