# Release decisions and ownership

Recorded 8 October 2026. These are unresolved human decisions, not approvals supplied
by software QA. The repository permits Offer and Ziv to merge changes; that does not
appoint either person as data controller, branding rights holder or screening operator.

| Decision | Responsible assignment | Review deadline | Status and required evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contributor/AI attribution | Maintainers to assign with contributor consent | Before source freeze or public release | Attribution form names and scope confirmed by the team |
| Scientific claims and model acceptance | Team to name scientific approver | Before efficacy/calibration claims | Independent evaluation, model/reference versions and human review |
| Human transcriptomic data, retention and post-competition accounts | Team/institution to appoint data steward/controller | Before accepting public uploads | Approved retention periods, deletion/export process, consent and account policy |
| Deployment, backup/restore and sequence screening operations | Maintainers to appoint operational owner | Before public deployment or release | Environment verification and recovery/screening evidence |
| CERNAL design assets and team photographs | Team to appoint rights/consent reviewer | Before public source/wiki publication | Asset provenance, license and individual photo consent |
| AIS-China branding/logo and collaboration link | Team to appoint collaboration contact | Before displaying a collaborator logo | Written trademark/logo permission; published collaboration source link |
| Python/R/MATLAB distribution publication | Maintainers to appoint release owner | Before registry publication | Tested distributions, supported runtimes, version/license metadata and authorization |
| Ordering/synthesis integration | Team to appoint funded partner owner | Before enabling ordering | Partner contract, integration acceptance and explicit authorization |
| Bundled/public dataset provenance and refresh | Team to appoint data steward | Before advertising biological contrasts | Provider metadata, checksums, exact contrasts and illustrative labeling |

Until those assignments and decisions are recorded, public release governance remains
open. No retention duration or post-competition account deletion promise has been
invented. Uploaded data should only be accepted under an approved institutional policy.
The synthetic example is explicitly illustrative; public provider tables retain their
source provenance but still need biological contrast review.

Researcher notes and tags are part of the existing local review workflow. Shortlisting
records a research decision; it does not authorize sequence release or synthesis.
Ordering remains planned/unavailable. R/MATLAB clients remain experimental until
runtime conformance passes. Packages are installed from source in this checkout;
no PyPI/CRAN/MATLAB publication is claimed.
